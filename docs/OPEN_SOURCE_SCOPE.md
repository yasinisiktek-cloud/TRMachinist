# Open-source scope

This repository publishes the TRMachinist simulation source while intentionally excluding data that may be confidential, proprietary, licensed separately or unnecessary for source review.

## Included

- TRMachinist-authored C# and XAML source.
- Triple-dexel/IPW engine.
- G-code/controller parsing infrastructure.
- Kinematics and coordinate transforms.
- Collision and tool/holder geometry infrastructure.
- Self-contained tests.
- Synthetic NC examples.

## Not included

- customer or production NC programs,
- real production CAD/mesh/job packages,
- commercial postprocessor source,
- Siemens NX binaries or NXOpen redistributables,
- machine-builder confidential packages or PLC data,
- private development evidence containing workstation paths or customer identifiers.

Vendor names may appear where needed to describe compatibility or parser behavior; those names and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.
