# Code of Conduct

TRMachinist welcomes technical discussion from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Be respectful, discuss the engineering rather than the person, document assumptions, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats or publication of another person's private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive, off-topic or unsafe content and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project constructive.

For CNC-specific discussions, disagreement about controller behavior should be resolved with reproducible evidence, public documentation where possible, and explicit machine/controller scope.
